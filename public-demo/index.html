Explore the sample workspace

  1. Choose a career field, workplace, or salary filter.
  2. Open More filters for experience, job type, industry, location, travel, interview steps, or application status.
  3. Combine filters to narrow results. Clear an individual filter using its chip, or clear them all.
  4. Open a numbered card, edit its sample workflow, and check the filtered pipeline.
  5. Export a CSV to keep those sample edits.

Everything here is fictional. Nothing you type is sent to an application backend or saved between visits.

Sample interview-step counts do not predict employer turnaround, interview invitations or offer probabilities. Unknown values are excluded when you set a numeric limit.

The full private workspace also supports a Perplexity research handoff and validated imports. Those are not automatic job-search or hiring services.
