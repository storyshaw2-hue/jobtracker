Employer source ledger

The server checks allowlisted public employer ATS feeds. An API listing is evidence at its check time, not a guarantee that a job stays open.

Country filters use country information stated in the feed or location. Sector and seniority labels are groupings; confirm the full employer requirements before applying.

Use the real-job workspace

  1. Load current employer feeds and review the Sources ledger.
  2. Combine filters, then open the employer posting to confirm all eligibility and role details.
  3. Save a role, update its status, and write your next action.
  4. Export your workflow before closing this tab. Reloading clears tab-only changes.

Public listings are pulled automatically; there is no fictional fallback. Missing salary, travel and interview-process information stays unknown. An hourly or non-USD amount does not pass an annual USD floor.

Use the Perplexity job agent with your actual resumes for private, evidence-grounded matching. Match/readiness scores are not hiring probabilities, and interview-step counts do not establish a fast offer.

Refreshing listings preserves current tab workflow. A tracked role missing from a later feed is retained for rechecking, not silently deleted or declared closed.

Reset this tab's workflow?

This clears only your current tab's saved flags, statuses, dates, notes and next actions. It does not change employer listings or your separate private tracker.
